# Writing rules for anything Chase will send

Applies to emails, messages, posts, and documents for other people.

1. **Never invent facts.** Use only facts Chase has given. No made-up metrics, tools, titles, dates, or experience. If something the reader wants is missing, flag it as a gap and ask; don't fill it in.
2. **Human voice.** Concrete, specific, varied sentence openings and lengths. Avoid AI-sounding words: spearheaded, leveraged, utilized, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, dynamic, passionate, synergy, delve, tapestry, fostered, showcasing, pivotal, transformative, meticulous, innovative, results-driven, streamlined, orchestrated, championed, empowered.
3. **No dashes** in prose: no em dashes, en dashes, or hyphenated compounds where a rewrite works ("five month" or "a team of three" instead of "five-month", "3-person"). Proper names and standard terms can keep theirs (e.g. ADS-B in a skills list).
4. **Concise.** Short and specific. Cut filler, throat-clearing, and clichés ("hardworking professional", "proven track record", "I hope this finds you well").
